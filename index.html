Ben Long

Very short generic sword guy. Honest, simple, and painfully default.

Keyboard

P1: WASD for movement/shield. J attack, K special, L grab, ; dodge.

P2: Arrow keys for movement/shield. Z attack, X special, C grab, V dodge.

Rules

Build damage, launch opponents offstage, and take all 3 stocks. Hold a direction while attacking or using special for side, up, down, and air moves. Tap jump for short hops, hold down in the air to fast fall, grab ledges to recover, grabs beat shields, and dodges give brief invulnerability.
